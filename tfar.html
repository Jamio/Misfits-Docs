Installation, radio setup, controls and communications tips for TFAR.

Installation and pre-flight check

  1. Install the Teamspeak 3 client.
  2. Subscribe to and load Task Force Arrowhead Radio (BETA!!!) in the Arma launcher.
  3. Open the TFAR mod folder from the launcher's Workshop mod options, enter its teamspeak folder, and run the included .ts3_plugin installer.
  4. In TeamSpeak, confirm the Task Force Arrowhead Radio plugin is enabled.
  5. Join the Misfits TeamSpeak server before launching the operation and complete a local/radio check in game.

Use the TFAR version supplied by the current Misfits modset. Mixing the old stable mod, beta mod, plugin versions or TeamSpeak installations is a common source of failure.

If you are struggling at any step, take a look at Wolfies handy TFAR installation guide from the year 2005...

How TFAR communication works

Direct speech

Your normal voice is positional and only heard nearby. Adjust speaking volume when you need to whisper, talk normally or project your voice.

Short-range radio

Usually carried in the radio inventory slot. It is intended for your section, fireteam or other local tactical net.

Long-range radio

Usually carried as a backpack or built into a vehicle. It is intended for command, support and communication between separated elements.

Terrain, distance, obstructions, radio type and antenna settings can affect reception. A radio is not a guarantee that every transmission will be clear.

Configuring a radio

Open a radio through ACE Self Interaction → Radios → your radio → Open, or use the configured radio-interface keybind. Radio faces differ, but the same concepts apply. Refer to the diagram below to get a run through of what a typical radio interface looks like:

  1. Volume Control - Left and right licking this will cycle the volume in increments of 10.
  2. Current Channel - Displays the current channel your radio is set to.
  3. Frequency - Displays the current frequency on the selected channel.
  4. Clear Frequency - Clicking this will clear whatever frequency is currently entered.
  5. Next/Previous Channel - Cycles through all available channels.
  6. Speakers/Headphones - Cycles between playing the radio out loud or just to you. Keep this on headphones unless you know what you're doing.
  7. Set Additional Channel - Clicking this will set the current channel as an Alternative channel, which allows you to listen/speak on that frequency with specific keybinds.
  8. Stereo Settings - Cycles through left, right and both to direct playback to either of your ears.

Additional channel

A radio can monitor a second channel. Select the desired secondary channel, set it as additional, configure its stereo side, then return the display to your primary channel. Use the separate additional-channel transmit key when you need to speak on it.

Good radio practice

  • Listen before transmitting and keep your calls concise and clear
  • Where possible, identify who you are and who you are trying to reach.
  • Leave command and support frequencies clear of unnecessary chatter.
  • Use direct speech inside the team, unless a radio transmission is needed.
  • If you change channels or frequencies, let others around you know who might've missed the message.

Vehicle radios and intercoms

Many vehicles provide built-in long-range radios. Radio configuration can be tied to a specific turret or seat, so moving from pilot to co-pilot—or between crew positions—may expose a different radio that must be configured separately.

Vehicle intercom is internal positional communication, not a transmitted radio net. Use ACE interaction to join the appropriate crew, passenger or intercom channel. If you can hear the world but not your crew, check the intercom channel before changing radio frequencies.

Troubleshooting

  • Is TeamSpeak 3 running and connected to the correct server?
  • Is the TFAR plugin enabled, and does its version match the loaded Arma mod?
  • Did Arma move you into the TaskForceRadio channel?
  • Do you actually possess the appropriate short- or long-range radio?
  • Are you on the assigned channel and frequency?
  • Is the radio switched on, audible, and routed to the expected ear?
  • Did you change vehicle seats and begin using a different onboard radio?
  • Are your TFAR addon keybinds conflicting with another control?

If TFAR still fails, restart TeamSpeak and Arma after confirming the plugin installation.
