Misfits Aux

The permanent Misfits addon: quality-of-life features, medical extensions, mission-making tools and unit content available across every campaign.

01

ACE BFT Customisation

Players · Leaders

Group leaders can change their ACE Blue Force Tracking marker icon and colour while the mission is running.

Access
ACE Self Interaction → Team Management → Edit BFT Marker.
Persists
The chosen marker is replicated to other players and retained for join-in-progress clients.

Expand later: add a screenshot of the selector and any group policy for marker shapes or colours.

02

CH View Distance

Players

The familiar CH View Distance interface provides separate view and object-distance settings for infantry, ground vehicles, aircraft and connected UAVs.

Configuration
Use the existing CHVD keybind and interface.
Compatibility
Existing CHVD profile settings and customised keybinds are retained.
03

Backpack on Chest

Players

Move a loaded backpack onto your chest when another backpack slot item—such as a parachute or long-range radio—must be worn on your back.

Use
Available through the familiar Backpack on Chest interactions.
Inventory
The stored backpack and its contents are preserved through supported swaps and transitions.
04

Prone Launcher

Players

Supported launchers can be aimed and fired while prone, providing a lower firing position than vanilla Arma normally permits.

Camera note: first person remains the most reliable view. The custom third-person offset can also influence the prone-launcher camera.

05

Ladder Tweak Remastered

Players

Improves ladder traversal with faster climbing animations and a configurable slide-down action.

Expand later: document the active slide keybind once the preferred group binding is standardised.

06

Third-person Camera

Players

Repositions the infantry third-person camera into the Misfits Ghost Recon-style shoulder view. Vehicle cameras retain their own definitions.

Server rules
The component does not override difficulty settings that disable third person.
Scope
Infantry camera positioning only.
07

Survivable Wrecks

Players · Mission makers

Aircraft and supported vehicles can enter a controlled crash sequence instead of always exploding immediately, giving crews a chance to survive the impact.

Settings
Crash thresholds, crew protection, unconsciousness, injuries, exclusions and wreck destruction are configurable through CBA settings.
Exceptions
Drones, AI, grounded vehicles and named classes can be treated differently.
08

Turret Tweaks

Mission makers

Reduces the laser-like accuracy of AI-operated vehicle turrets by adjusting the dispersion coefficients that reliably influence them.

Settings
CBA coefficients allow the desired dispersion to be tuned instead of relying on one hardcoded value.
Compatibility
Separate patches cover vanilla and only the vehicle mods present in the loaded modset.
09

Map Labels

Eden · Zeus

Add and remove named map locations with Eden or Zeus modules. Labels can mark objectives, settlements, bases and other mission-specific locations.

Eden
Find the add/remove modules under Map Locations.
Zeus
Place the corresponding modules and complete the label dialog during play.
10

Multiplayer Object Scaler

Eden

Adds a multiplayer-safe object-scale attribute to Eden and restores scaling for saved missions and join-in-progress clients.

Scaled simulated objects may need to be frozen or attached to remain reliable in multiplayer. Test unusual objects on a dedicated server.

11

Misfits Objects and Insignia

Eden · Zeus · Arsenal

Provides unit-themed flags, banners, artwork, signs, empty ammunition crates, rugged plastic cases and wearable insignia.

Objects
Look under the Misfits editor and Zeus categories.
Insignia
Available through compatible arsenal and insignia-selection interfaces.

Expand later: add a visual catalogue when the object collection grows enough to justify one.

12

Misfits Medical Systems

Players · Medics

The Aux extends ACE with the Misfits airway and breathing loop, SpO₂, revised PAK requirements, new treatments, deployable medical supplies, stretchers and automatic conversion of retrieved vanilla medical kits.

This is a connected gameplay system rather than a list of isolated items, so its full treatment procedure lives in one dedicated guide.

Open the complete medical guide

Key additions
Airway Tube, Pulse Oximeter, Emergency Fluid Bag, TXA, Universal Antagonist and Medical Aid Pack.
PAK
Eligible wounds must be bandaged and stitched, fractures treated, and the casualty stabilised before the action appears.
13

Recreational Items

Players

Misfits beer and Joint Operations packs apply escalating five-stage visual, audio and movement effects. Repeated use increases the stage; the final stage ends with brief unconsciousness.

These are joke gameplay items, not part of the medical treatment loop.

14

Radio Backpack

Players · TFAR

Provides the Misfits TFAR backpack-radio content used by supported loadouts and missions.

15

Framework Integration

Optional

Small compatibility components add Misfits identity, objects and operation-stat export when JM Framework is present. The Aux remains usable without the Framework, and the Framework remains independent of the Aux.

Operations export
Runs only for the authoritative Framework debrief when explicitly enabled.
Campaign content
WWII and 2041 campaign-specific systems remain in their separate campaign packs.

Open the separate JM Framework documentation →

Dependencies stay local

Each PBO depends only on the systems it actually extends. Medical and object components do not acquire TFAR, ZEN or 3den Enhanced dependencies merely because those mods are supported elsewhere. Optional compatibility components load only when their target mod is present.
