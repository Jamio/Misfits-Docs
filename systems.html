Universal systems

What the Aux changes.

These components belong to the permanent unit addon and are available regardless of campaign era.

Put here: one plain-English section per feature: what players notice, how to use it, any keybind, and important limitations. Keep implementation details in the private source repository.

ACE BFT

Group leaders can change their BFT marker icon and colour through ACE self-interaction.

CH View Distance

Per-context view and object distance controls with the familiar CHVD keybind and saved settings.

Backpack on Chest

Move a loaded backpack to the chest, swap bags, and retain inventory through supported transitions.

Prone Launcher

Use supported launchers while prone. First person remains the most reliable view with custom third-person offsets.

Ladder Tweak

Faster ladder movement and a configurable slide-down action.

Third-person camera

Ghost Recon-style infantry camera positioning while respecting servers that disable third person.

Survivable Wrecks

Configurable aircraft and vehicle crash-survival behaviour instead of immediate destruction.

Map Labels

Eden and Zeus modules for creating and removing map locations during missions.

Object Scaler

Multiplayer-safe Eden object scaling, including restoration for saved missions and join-in-progress clients.

Turret Tweaks

Dispersion adjustments for AI-operated turrets, split into isolated vanilla and mod compatibility PBOs.

Misfits objects

Flags, banners, artwork, signs, crates, plastic cases, and insignia for mission makers.

Recreational items

Beer and Joint Operations packs use escalating five-stage effects. Stage five lasts briefly before unconsciousness.

Dependencies stay local

A component only loads the systems it actually uses. For example, TFAR is not required by medical or object PBOs, while the Immersive Cigs integration is skipped when that mod is absent.
