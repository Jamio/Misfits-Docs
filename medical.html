Guide · ACE Medical

ACE / Misfits Medical

A practical guide to keeping casualties alive, understanding our medical loop, and using the additions supplied by Misfits Aux.

Orientation

What players need to know

ACE Medical can look intimidating, but our loop is deliberately focused: stop bleeding, protect the airway, restore circulation, treat injuries, and hand the patient to a medic for definitive care.

Everyone

  • Make the scene safe and move the casualty into cover.
  • Stop catastrophic bleeding and bandage wounds.
  • Check airway, breathing, pulse and blood pressure.
  • Call a medic early and clearly report what you found.

Medics

  • Coordinate treatment instead of doing everything alone.
  • Restore blood volume and manage medication.
  • Stitch bandaged wounds and splint fractures.
  • Use the PAK only after the patient meets our stability rules.

Reading the ACE medical menu

Select a body part to see wounds and available treatments. Red body regions need attention; darker red usually means more severe injury. Available actions depend on the selected body part, medical permissions, carried items, and the patient's condition.

Golden rule: An absent treatment is usually conditional, not broken. Check the selected limb, required item, medic level, patient state, and whether an earlier treatment is still required.

Field procedure

Treat in this order

  1. Secure and moveSuppress the threat and drag or carry the casualty into cover.
  2. Check response and major bleedingConfirm they are alive and rapidly inspect every body part.
  3. Control catastrophic bleedingTourniquet badly bleeding limbs when speed matters, then bandage wounds.
  4. Open the airwayUse head extension, head turning, recovery position, or an Airway Tube when required.
  5. Check breathingLook for normal, shallow, or absent breathing. Use the pulse oximeter when available.
  6. Restore circulationCheck pulse and blood pressure. Start CPR if pulseless; medics should replace lost volume.
  7. Remove tourniquetsOnce wounds beneath them are bandaged, remove tourniquets and recheck bleeding.
  8. Treat pain and fracturesUse medication carefully and splint fractured limbs.
  9. Stitch woundsA medic uses the Surgical Kit to make bandaged wounds permanent.
  10. Definitive treatmentOnce all configured requirements are met, the PAK action appears.
Non-medic quick loopCover → tourniquet → bandage → airway → CPR if needed → call medic
Patient state

What “stable” actually means

Conscious

Conscious does not mean healthy. Recheck bleeding, blood pressure, pain, fractures, breathing and medication.

Unconscious

Protect the airway, control blood loss, restore circulation and oxygenation, then allow time to recover.

Cardiac arrest

No pulse means CPR. CPR alone cannot compensate for uncontrolled bleeding or critically low blood volume.

Stable for PAK

With our defaults: alive and conscious, no bleeding, stable circulation, all treatable wounds bandaged and stitched, and fractures splinted. Bruises do not block the PAK.

Blood volume

6.0 L Normal5.1 L Stable threshold4.2 L Serious loss3.6 L Severe loss3.0 L Critical

ACE starts a healthy patient at approximately 6 litres. Our default stability requirement is roughly 85%, or 5.1 litres.

Why are they still unconscious?

  • Is bleeding fully controlled?
  • Do they have enough blood volume?
  • Do they have a pulse and acceptable blood pressure?
  • Is the airway managed and are they breathing?
  • Is SpO₂ above the recovery threshold?
  • Are medication effects suppressing recovery?

Tourniquets

Immediately stop limb bleeding, but are temporary. Bandage underneath, remove the tourniquet, then recheck. Pain begins to build after roughly two minutes.

Stitching

Bandages close wounds temporarily. A Surgical Kit permanently closes bandaged wounds and is required by our PAK rules.

Fractures

Splints restore useful movement. With our default settings, untreated fractures block the PAK.

Medication

Check heart rate first, allow time to act, and avoid stacking doses blindly. Morphine lowers heart rate; epinephrine raises it.

Respiratory care

Airway, breathing and SpO₂

Misfits Medical provides a compact KAT-style loop without oxygen tanks, pneumothorax, chest drains or a full respiratory simulation.

1

Check Airway

Establish whether the airway is clear, obstructed or occluded.

2

Head extension / turning

Fast manual manoeuvres that can clear an airway, but do not provide lasting protection.

3

Recovery position

A more sustained manual method for maintaining the airway while the patient remains positioned.

4

Airway Tube

The strongest and most durable intervention. It also improves SpO₂ recovery.

Pulse Oximeter

Provides an accurate SpO₂ reading. Below 90% is dangerous and can prevent recovery.

Clear airway ≠ breathing. A patient in cardiac arrest or with critically poor circulation may still not breathe. Control bleeding, restore volume and perform CPR while keeping the airway managed.

Equipment

Core and Misfits medical items

IV

Blood, plasma and saline

Restore circulating volume. Blood is preferred; smaller bags avoid unnecessary waste.

Rx

Morphine and epinephrine

Morphine manages pain and lowers heart rate. Epinephrine raises heart rate. Neither replaces blood.

SK

Surgical Kit and PAK

The Surgical Kit stitches wounds. The PAK only appears once our configured readiness rules are met.

TXA Injector

A medic-only treatment that modestly reduces blood loss for a limited time. It does not close wounds.

UA

Universal Antagonist

A medic-only emergency treatment that clears active medication burden. Medics may self-administer it.

100

Emergency Fluid Bag

A 100 mL emergency volume stopgap usable by non-medics.

Medical Aid Pack

A deployable, persistent supply case. Deployment takes time; repacking preserves its contents.

Troubleshooting

When something will not work

The PAK action is missing

Check consciousness, active bleeding, blood volume and circulation, stitched wounds, and splinted fractures. Bruises are deliberately ignored.

A treatment is not listed

Select the correct body part, confirm you carry the item, check your permission level, and verify the patient's condition makes it valid.

The airway is clear but the patient is not breathing

Airway and breathing are separate. Check pulse, blood pressure, blood volume and SpO₂. Continue CPR when pulseless.

The patient keeps reopening wounds

Bandages are temporary. Stitch every bandageable wound, particularly after using Elastic bandages.

Treatment appears stuck

Stop competing interactions, let the current progress action finish, and keep patient and medic in a valid position. If it remains broken, tell Zeus and retain the client RPT.
