Medical guide

Airway, breathing, circulation.

Misfits Medical adds a compact KAT-style airway and breathing loop without oxygen tanks, chest drains, pneumothorax, or a full respiratory simulation.

Unconscious patient workflow

  1. Control major bleeding and check pulse.
  2. Check Airway. If obstructed, extend or turn the head, use the recovery position, or insert an Airway Tube.
  3. Check Breathing. The result is normal, shallow, or not breathing.
  4. Use Pulse Oximeter for an exact SpO2 reading.
  5. Treat circulation: CPR is only useful once the airway is open, and cardiac arrest still needs the normal ACE response.
  6. Bandage, stitch all bandageable wounds, splint fractures, and stabilise the patient before using a PAK.

Manual airway manoeuvres

Head extension/turning are quick, item-free temporary measures. Recovery position is useful for an unconscious casualty who can be left safely positioned. Reassess after movement or deterioration.

Airway Tube

The reliable item-based option for clearing and maintaining the airway. It is deliberately simpler than carrying several overlapping KAT airway devices.

SpO2 and waking up

An open airway does not guarantee breathing. Breathing also depends on viable circulation and the patient's medical state. A casualty with no pulse or zero blood pressure will not recover merely because their airway is clear. Prolonged failure to breathe lowers SpO2, and severe hypoxia prevents consciousness.

Special items

Misfits Serum

A mild heart-rate increase plus a short visual and movement-speed effect. It is not a real stabilisation drug.

Essential Oils

Consumes the item, logs the treatment, and delivers wellness wisdom. It changes no medical state whatsoever.

Vanilla kit conversion

Retrieved First Aid Kits and Medikits are converted into the configured ACE supplies, including bandages, medication, fluids, and surgical equipment.

Do not load Misfits Airway alongside KAT Airway. The Misfits implementation is intended as the group's compact alternative.
