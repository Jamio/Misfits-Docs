Getting started

The practical route from joining the group to being ready for an operation.

Welcome

Before your first operation

Welcome to Misfits Arma. Follow these steps to understand how we organise and play our operations.

  1. Download the current modlist

    The modlist for operations is posted in advance either within the #mission-sign-up channel on Discord, or linked within the briefing document posted in #mission-info. Download the .html file and drag it over the open Arma 3 Launcher. This subscribes to the required mods and creates a preset in the launcher dropdown.

  2. Ensure you have the correct cDLCs

    If the modlist is imported correctly, the launcher should also select the relevant DLC. If you do not own a required DLC, a compatibility-data mod may let you join while limiting access to its content. Compatibility data cannot provide DLC terrains, so you cannot join an operation running on terrain you do not own.

  3. Read the player guides

    The guides on this site explain our major systems and how we expect them to be used. Separate JM Framework documentation covers the recurring mission systems you may encounter.

    Browse the player guides →

  4. Set up TFAR

    TFAR simulates local voice and radio communication instead of relying on Arma's basic VoIP. Install the TeamSpeak plugin and understand the essential controls before mission night.

    Open the TFAR setup and usage guide →

  5. Load into the server

    If this is your first time playing Arma or installing mods, ask somebody to start the server so you can confirm that the preset loads without errors. Raise problems in Discord while there is still time to fix them.

  6. Check your controls and settings

    Arma has an enormous number of keybinds and combinations. Familiarise yourself with stances, freelook, firing, reloading, vehicles, storage inventories and addon controls. If possible, ask an experienced player to run through the essentials with you.

  7. Familiarise yourself with ACE

    ACE3 touches almost every part of the game. Practise interaction and self-interaction on players, vehicles and objects, and make sure you understand the foundations of our medical system.

    Open the complete medical guide →

  8. Test TFAR in game

    Once connected, test direct speech, radios, frequencies, speaking volume, stereo routing and any controls you are unsure about. You should be confident that you can take a radio and join your squad's frequency.

Mission night

Your first operation

Roles and teams

Roles are typically selected in advance through the mission-maker's briefing document posted in #mission-info. As a team member, listen to your immediate leadership and support the people around you.

Briefing

Briefings are posted before the mission and may contain vital information. Read the objectives, opposition, rules of engagement, support, resupply, reinsertion and information specific to your role. Pay attention to the in-game briefing as well.

Loadouts and resupply

Your kit may be assigned or built from a role-restricted arsenal. Check weapons, magazines, medical supplies, navigation tools and radios. Resupply is based on authorised starting equipment, so an enemy weapon may be difficult to replenish later.

Respawn and reinsertion

Respawn normally returns you to a staging area. Regroup and resupply before using the available rally point, squadmate deployment or transport request.

You do not need to memorise every system. You do need to know your basic controls, communicate when unsure, and arrive prepared enough that the operation can start.
