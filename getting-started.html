Welcome

Introduction

Welcome to Misfits Arma. Follow this guide to understand how we organise and play our operations.

  1. There is not much point in starting treatment if you are still exposed or taking direct fire. Your first priority should be to secure the area - focus on making it safe to begin treatment, and moving your patient to cover if possible.
  2. Download the current modlist The modlist for operations is posted in advance either within the #mission-sign-up channel on Discord, or linked within the briefing document that gets posted in #mission-info. To install the modlist, download the .html file, and drag said file over your Arma 3 Launcher while its open. This will automatically subscribe to all necessary mods and populate a preset list for you that is available via the dropdown menu in the top right of the launcher.
  3. Ensure you have the correct cDLCs If you have downlaoded the modlist correctly, the launcher should also load the relevant DLCs automatically. If you do not own a necssary DLC, you may be able to use one of the compatibility mods in its place. These will allow you to join the server without owning the DLC - you will get watermarks for using DLC content. However, if the terrain being used for an operation is from a DLC, you will not be able to join, as terrains are not included in the compatibility mods.
  4. Read the Player Guides The guides are included elsewhere in this site, and will give you an idea of how the systems we use interact with eachother, and how you are expected to use them. There are also guides for the mission framework we often use that will explain how the systems within that also work.
  5. Setup TFAR TFAR is a mod that helps simulate local voice and radio communications within arma (the default voip is a bit meh...). It takes a bit of setting up but shouldnt take long at all. Make sure you understand how it works - we have an install and usage guide in this site.
  6. Load into the server If it is your first time playing Arma, or first time installing mods - ask someone to set the server up for you and check that you can join it without errors. If you have any issues, ask in Discord.
  7. Check your controls & settings This may seem obvious, but Arma has a ridiculous number of keybinds and key combinations for different actions. Take your time on the server and familiarise yourself with things like stances, freelook, firing, reloading, entering vehicles, interacting with storage inventories etc. If possible, grab someone who is familiar with the game to join you and run through things.
  8. Familiarise yourself with ACEACE3 is a major overhaul mod that touches just about every part of the game, and we use it for a LOT of things. You should already be aware of ACE by going through the player guides. Whilst you are in the server, take the time to familiarise yourself with how ACE works - try things like pointing, using interation menus on yourself, others and objects. Also, make sure you understand how to use ACE's medical system, and our settings which are also included in a player guide.
  9. Test TFARYou should already have TFAR set up by this point. Now you are on the server, take the opportunity to try out direct speech, radios, radio frequencies, whispering/talking/shouting, volume, stereo settings and anything else you are unsure about. You should be confident that you can grab a radio and get on the same frequency as your squad.

Your first operation

Roles and teams

Roles are selected in advance or in the mission lobby. Listen to your team leader, remain with your element, communicate, and help the people around you. Nobody benefits from an isolated player requiring a separate rescue mission.

Briefing

Open the map and read the briefing tabs. They may contain objectives, enemy strength, civilian presence, rules of engagement, support, resupply and reinsertion information.

Loadouts and resupply

Your kit may be assigned or built from a role-restricted arsenal. Check weapons, magazines, medical supplies, navigation tools and radios. Resupply is based on authorised starting equipment, so an enemy weapon may be difficult to replenish later.

Respawn and reinsertion

Respawn normally returns you to a staging area. Regroup and resupply before using the available rally point, squadmate deployment or transport request. Do not immediately deploy into an unsafe rally.

Framework player tools

Grass cutter

Use ACE Self Interaction → Equipment → Grasscutter to flatten a small patch of grass obstructing a firing position. It is a visibility tool, not a method for removing scenery.

Earplugs

Use the available earplug action during loud vehicle rides or sustained firing. They reduce game sound while keeping TFAR voices intelligible; remember to remove them when situational audio matters.

You do not need to memorise every system. You do need to know your basic controls, communicate when unsure, and arrive prepared enough that the operation can start.
