Playing Framework Missions

A player reference for the common systems used across Misfits operations.

The JM Mission Framework gives mission makers a consistent set of configurable systems. Every mission uses a different selection, so an option described here may not always be available.

01
Mission information

Briefings

Framework missions can provide information before the game begins and through organised map briefing entries.

What may be provided

The loading briefing may introduce the situation, while map entries can cover objectives, friendly and enemy forces, civilians, rules of engagement, signals, logistics and available support.

How players use it

Read the lobby briefing before selecting Ready, then open the map briefing once loaded. Check it again whenever you need frequencies, callsigns, restrictions or objective details.

02
Permissions and responsibilities

Roles

Your lobby slot defines your group and role. The Framework can attach qualifications that control access to particular equipment or actions.

Role options

Common qualifications include leadership, medic, engineer, explosive specialist, JTAC, pilot, crew and logistics. A mission can use only the roles it needs.

How players use it

Check the role card shown after deployment and understand your responsibilities. If Zeus changes your role during play, supported permissions and systems update to match.

03
Starting equipment

Loadouts

Missions can issue a complete loadout or allow players to build one through a role-restricted ACE Arsenal.

Loadout options

A fixed loadout is prepared entirely by the mission maker. A restricted arsenal combines common equipment with items allowed for your role, such as medic supplies or specialist weapons.

How players use it

Check weapons, ammunition, medical supplies, navigation equipment and radios before leaving. Where persistence is enabled, the loadout saved when you close the arsenal is restored after respawn.

04
Sustainment

Resupplies

Supply points provide mission-approved crates without giving players unrestricted arsenal access.

Supply options

The mission may offer ammunition, medical, engineering, CSW or custom crates. Available definitions, access permissions and quantities are configured for that operation.

How players use it

Interact with a marked supply point, select an available crate and request it. The crate may immediately enter ACE carrying so it can be moved or loaded into a vehicle. Weapons outside the planned loadout may not be supported by resupply.

05
Returning to the operation

Redeployment

When enabled, the deployment station provides controlled methods for returning respawned players to their unit.

Squad Rally

An eligible squad leader places a rally for their group. Nearby enemies can block its use.

Platoon Rally

A designated platoon leader places a rally available to the wider force.

Squadmate

Some missions allow deployment to a living group member when their position is safe.

Pickup Request

Sends a request for physical reinsertion by pilots, logistics or another transport element.

Player procedure: regroup and resupply, contact your element, then use the deployment station. Confirm that the destination is still appropriate before deploying.

06
Player lifecycle

Death and Respawning

The mission maker controls whether players respawn, how many lives are available and what happens after death.

Possible rules

Respawn may be unlimited, controlled by team tickets, delayed or disabled through permadeath. The mission can use a death camera or spectator mode while a player waits.

How players use it

ACE unconsciousness is not death, so remain available for treatment. After respawning, recover your role and loadout, regroup at the designated area and use the configured redeployment system.

07
Mission-dependent features

Special Systems

These systems appear only when the mission maker enables and configures them.

JTAC

Qualified players can access the support terminal and any aircraft, artillery or other support made available for their side.

Garage

A garage provides a controlled catalogue of mission vehicles. Some garages also define nearby repair, refuel and rearm facilities.

Fortify

Eligible players can spend a configured construction budget on approved defensive objects within the allowed area.

Unknown Weapons

The Framework can warn when an unauthorised weapon is fired and apply configured accuracy, jamming, reload or failure penalties. Use weapons provided by the mission wherever possible.

Intel

Mission objects can contain collectable intelligence. Recovered information may update tasks, markers or the wider situation.

Forestry

ACE self-interactions can clear grass, remove a nearby bush or fell a targeted tree. The permitted actions, tools and timings are set by the mission.

CBRN

CBRN missions can use contaminated areas, protective-equipment checks and exposure effects. Follow the briefing for required protection and procedures.

Aid Stations

Medics may deploy an aid station that provides supplies or a recognised medical-facility position. Deployment takes time and may be role restricted.

Vehicle Service

Repair, refuel and rearm stations service vehicles inside their configured area. Not every workshop object automatically provides service.

Earplugs

Framework earplugs reduce game audio while preserving radio communication and retain their state across arsenal use.

Screenshot Mode

A player action hides the vanilla HUD and supported overlays such as DUI. Use the exit action to restore the interface.

World Labels

Configured objects and locations can display labels identifying supply points, deployment stations or other important features.

Mission-making documentation

This page describes player interaction only. Component setup, Eden modules, CBA settings and scripting are documented in the JM Framework Docs.
