About the Misfits

The most hardcore, serious, organised milsim group on the internet.

Who we are

We play semi-realistic cooperative Arma without sacrificing fun. Our missions range from stealth infiltrations and sabotage, to hearts and minds, thunder runs, combined arms assaults, combat patrols and so much more. We tend to engage in long-form campaigns within a setting for extended periods of time.

Misfits Arma was founded in 2022 off the back of a dead and dying HLL clan. We discovered that we really enjoyed being able to craft experiences in Arma 3, and have been playing ever since - enjoying immersing ourselves in diffferent scenarios, working together with our teammates,a and pulling off some unbelievable feats of heroism in the process.

What we expect

We expect all of our players to engage with the mission, work together, and make the whole experience work for everyone. We dont take things too seriously, but we do enjoy a good bit of tryharding and roleplay.

Rules

We only have a few rules, as we like to keep things open. We expect that our players know where the boundaries are, and dont jeoporadise the experience for others:

  1. Engage with the missions and the people around you. Staying immersed in the mission improves the experience a thousand-fold.
  2. Communicate - with your teammates, with other players, with leaders. The whole premise of our Arma sessions is built on being able to communicate effectively in the heat of battle, and under pressure.
  3. Provide feedback at the right time, and with the right attitude. Our group values constructive criticism and feedback - it has evolved our group over the years, but it must be given in a positive light, and not to tear others down or diminish the work of everyone involved.
  4. Be respectful, be kind, and respect the people around you who take time out of their days to create missions, maintain the server, and play the operations with you.

Thats pretty much it...
