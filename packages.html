Modset guide

One universal mod, optional campaign packs.

The old campaign-fixes bundles mixed permanent features with era-specific content. They are now separated.

Mission typeLoadProvides
Normal / modernMisfits AuxAll universal QOL, medical, objects, insignia, and supported compatibility components.
Misfits 2041Misfits Aux + Misfits 2041JCA/MCRP vests, Red Sigil gear, synths, campaign items/props, sensor HUD, menu, and optional Aegis flare patch.
Misfits WWIIMisfits Aux + Misfits WWIIPeriod scenery/props, WWII menu, BC-611 range patch, and IJA identity fix.

Things you no longer load from the old bundles

The campaign packs do not contain duplicate copies of CHVD, Backpack on Chest, ACE BFT customisation, Prone Launcher, Ladder Tweak, Multiplayer Scaler, Third-person Camera, Survivable Wrecks, radio-backpack additions, or other universal Aux systems.

External mods remain external

LAMBS, SER effects packages, KAT, Immersive Cigs, TFAR, ACE, terrain dependencies, faction mods, and DLC/content mods are still separate dependencies where a mission needs them. A compatibility PBO does not redistribute the mod it patches.

For current testing: disable the old @Jamio_2041_Fixes or @Jamio_WW2_Fixes bundle when testing its replacement. Loading both can duplicate class patches and hide migration problems.

Quick fault finding

  • Universal feature missing? Check Misfits Aux.
  • 2041 gear or presentation missing? Check Misfits 2041 and its campaign dependencies.
  • WWII prop or radio tweak missing? Check Misfits WWII and its campaign dependencies.
  • Only one optional compatibility feature missing? Confirm the third-party mod it patches is loaded.
